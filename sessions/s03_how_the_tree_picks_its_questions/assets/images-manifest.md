Generator rules (learned the hard way, now enforced in the script):
- Ball grids: never hard-code per-row counts or radii — geometry is derived from box size
  and ball count; ball radius is SHARED per figure (a 1-ball box must not grow a giant ball).
- RTL: ≥/≤ inside Persian text needs LRM marks (U+200E) around the math fragment — bidi
  isolates (U+2066/2069) are silently ignored by the kaleido render path (verified).
- Honesty: symbolic ball counts get a printed «توپ‌ها نمادین‌اند» disclaimer (titanic_champion).
- Titles: chart titles use xref/yref="paper", never data coordinates on categorical axes.
- After regenerating, LOOK at each PNG (overflow, mirrored operators, misplaced titles).
